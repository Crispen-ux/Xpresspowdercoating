Powder coating is a dry finishing process where electrostatically charged powder particles are sprayed onto a metal surface and then cured under heat or UV light. The powder melts, flows, and forms a tough, uniform, and durable finish that is resistant to chipping, scratching, fading, and corrosion. It is an environmentally friendly alternative to liquid paint as it produces no volatile organic compounds (VOCs).

When applied correctly, powder coating can last for many years without significant degradation. On average, a professional powder coat finish can last 15 to 20 years or more, depending on environmental conditions, the type of powder used, and the quality of surface preparation. In industrial and outdoor applications, powder coating outperforms conventional paint in terms of durability and longevity.

Powder coating is primarily used on metal surfaces including steel, iron, aluminium, and zinc. Common items that can be powder coated include driveway gates, burglar bars, security fencing, metal furniture, industrial components, school desks and chairs, bull bars, and more. Non-metallic materials such as MDF can also be powder coated with specialised processes.

Proper surface preparation is critical for a quality powder coat finish. Parts should be free of rust, oil, grease, and old paint. At Xpress Powder Coating, we handle preparation which may include degreasing, sandblasting, or chemical cleaning to ensure the powder adheres properly. If you are unsure about the condition of your parts, bring them to us and we will assess the best preparation method.

The turnaround time depends on the complexity and quantity of parts. Standard jobs typically take 2 to 5 business days from preparation to completion. Larger industrial projects or jobs requiring special colours or finishes may take longer. We always aim to deliver within the agreed timeframe and will provide an estimated completion date when you place your order.
