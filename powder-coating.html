What is Powder Coating?

An advanced finishing process that delivers superior results compared to traditional liquid paint.

Powder coating is a dry finishing process where finely ground particles of pigment and resin are electrostatically charged and sprayed onto a surface. The charged powder adheres to the grounded metal part, creating a uniform layer that is then cured under heat or UV light to form a tough, durable finish.

Unlike conventional liquid paint, powder coating contains no solvents and releases virtually zero volatile organic compounds (VOCs) into the atmosphere. This makes it the environmentally responsible choice for metal finishing while simultaneously producing a finish that is thicker, more consistent, and significantly more durable than standard paint.

The electrostatic application ensures the powder wraps around the part, coating surfaces that would be difficult to reach with conventional spray methods. This results in a uniform coat thickness without runs, sags, or drips.

Once cured at temperatures between 160°C and 200°C, the powder melts, flows, and chemically reacts to form a continuous, cross-linked film. The result is a finish that resists chipping, scratching, fading, and corrosion far better than traditional paint systems.

Benefits of Powder Coating

Why powder coating is the preferred finishing solution for metal products.

Exceptional Durability

Resists chipping, scratching, chemicals, UV rays, and corrosion. Outlasts liquid paint by years, even in harsh South African conditions.

Our team assesses the base material and selects the appropriate pre-treatment method — whether that is sandblasting, chemical cleaning, phosphating, or chromating — to guarantee the best possible result.

We also coat mixed-metal assemblies where different components require different preparation methods, ensuring a consistent finish across the entire product.

Our Powder Coating Process

A systematic approach to deliver consistent, high-quality results every time.

01

Surface Preparation

Sandblasting, degreasing, or chemical pre-treatment to remove rust, scale, and contaminants. A clean surface is critical for adhesion.

02

Masking

Precision masking of areas that must remain uncoated, such as threaded holes, mating surfaces, and electrical contact points.

03

Electrostatic Application

Dry powder is electrostatically charged and sprayed onto the grounded part for uniform coverage, including recessed areas and edges.

04

Curing

Parts are cured in our industrial oven at controlled temperatures. The powder melts, flows, and cross-links to form a tough, continuous film.

05

Quality Inspection

Every piece is inspected for coating thickness, adhesion, colour accuracy, and surface defects before being approved for dispatch.
