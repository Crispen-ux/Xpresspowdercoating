Our Story

Building on Experience Since 2017

Xpress Powder Coating is a 100% black-owned company formed in 2019, building on a strong foundation of work performed under Kuthula Consulting since 2017. Based in Springs, Benoni, in Ekurhuleni, Gauteng, we have grown to serve a diverse range of clients across the public and private sectors.

Our existence is driven by a commitment to create job opportunities in the communities where we operate, contributing to local economic development while delivering prompt, quality services that exceed our customers' expectations.

We have completed powder coating work for clients including Chemcraft, Care Drive, SA Post Office, Department of Water Affairs, Mowana Properties (PIC), TFM Industries, and numerous private clients.
