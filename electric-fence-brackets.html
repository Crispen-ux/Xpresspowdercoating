Electric Fence Brackets

We manufacture and supply a comprehensive range of electric fence brackets designed for the South African market.

Each bracket is produced from quality steel to ensure reliable performance and long-lasting durability in outdoor conditions. Our brackets are engineered to provide secure mounting for electric fence wires, insulators, and other fence components.

Whether you are installing a new electric fence or replacing brackets on an existing system, we have the right solution for your project.

What We Manufacture

  • Standard wall-mount electric fence brackets
  • Post-mount brackets for various post sizes
  • Custom brackets for specific fence configurations
  • Brackets compatible with all major electric fence systems
  • Bulk production for commercial and industrial installers
  • Replacement brackets for existing fencing installations

Quality Materials for South African Conditions

South Africa's climate demands materials that can withstand extreme heat, heavy rain, and harsh UV exposure.

Every bracket we produce is designed to resist corrosion and maintain structural integrity under demanding conditions. We focus on using the right steel grade and manufacturing processes to ensure each bracket delivers dependable service throughout the life of your electric fence system.

  • Quality steel construction for maximum strength
  • Weather-resistant to handle South African climate extremes
  • Corrosion-resistant for long outdoor service life
  • Precise manufacturing for consistent fit and performance
  • Suitable for high-security and industrial applications
  • Finished to resist rust and environmental wear
