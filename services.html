Transformer Radiator Manufacturing

Custom transformer radiator fabrication for power transformers, substations, and electrical distribution infrastructure. Our primary service.

We manufacture transformer radiator components and panels for power transformers used in electrical substations, power distribution networks, and industrial power infrastructure. Our fabrication capability supports transformer manufacturers, refurbishment companies, and infrastructure contractors throughout South Africa.

Transformer radiators are critical heat exchange components that maintain safe operating temperatures in oil-immersed power transformers. We fabricate radiator assemblies to specification with industrial powder coating finishes for corrosion resistance and durability.

Industrial Powder Coating

High-volume bulk powder coating for transformer radiators, electrical enclosures, distribution boards, and industrial components.

Our industrial powder coating service provides high-volume finishing for transformer radiators, electrical enclosures, distribution boards, and manufactured steel components. We serve manufacturers, engineering companies, and infrastructure contractors requiring consistent, durable finishes at production scale.

Powder coating provides superior corrosion resistance, UV stability, and mechanical durability compared to conventional liquid paint. It is the preferred finishing method for industrial components exposed to demanding operating environments.

Transformer Repairs

Complete transformer inspection, testing, repair, rewinds, and KVA upgrades.

We provide a full range of transformer repair and refurbishment services to restore your equipment to optimal operating condition. Our team has the expertise to handle everything from routine maintenance to complex core repairs and complete rewinds.

We work with all major transformer brands and can increase KVA ratings, change voltage configurations, and incorporate modern insulating materials to extend the lifespan of your transformers.

  • Refurbishment — Complete overhaul and restoration of transformer units

  • Restacking — Precision core restacking for improved efficiency
  • Rewinds — Professional coil rewinding for all transformer types
