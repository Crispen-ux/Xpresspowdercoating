Our Transformer Repair Services

Xpress Powder Coating delivers comprehensive transformer repair, refurbishment, and upgrade services to industrial and commercial clients throughout Springs, Johannesburg, and the greater Gauteng region. Our experienced team handles everything from routine inspections to full transformer rebuilds.

Inspection & Testing

Comprehensive diagnostic testing including insulation resistance, winding resistance, turns ratio, and oil analysis to identify faults and assess transformer condition accurately.

Core Repairs & Restacking

Expert core restacking, lamination repair, and realignment to restore magnetic efficiency. We repair damaged cores and replace degraded insulation between laminations.

Complete Rewinds

Full primary and secondary coil rewinds using high-grade copper or aluminium conductors. We manufacture custom windings to OEM specifications or modified requirements.

LTC Repairs & Adjustments

Load tap changer maintenance, contact refurbishment, and precise adjustments to ensure reliable voltage regulation under varying load conditions.

Full Refurbishment

Complete transformer refurbishment including tank repair, gasket replacement, bushing inspection, cooling system service, and external protective coating application.

KVA Upgrades & Voltage Changes

Increase your transformer capacity with KVA upgrades. We modify windings, update insulation systems, and upgrade clamping structures to handle increased power demands.

Transformer Upgrade Services

When your existing transformer can no longer meet operational demands, our upgrade services provide a cost-effective alternative to full replacement.

KVA Increases

We increase transformer capacity by rewinding with higher-rated conductors and upgrading insulation systems. Our team re-engineers the transformer to handle greater loads while maintaining safety and efficiency standards.

  • Capacity increases from 50KVA to 5MVA and beyond
  • Custom winding designs for specific load profiles
  • Thermal upgrades for sustained high-load operation

Voltage Changes

We modify transformer windings to accommodate voltage level changes required by system upgrades or equipment changes. All voltage conversions are performed to SANS and IEC standards.

  • Primary and secondary voltage modifications
  • Dual-voltage configurations
  • Tap winding adjustments for precision regulation

Clamping Structure Improvements

We reinforce and modernise transformer clamping structures to reduce vibration, improve mechanical strength, and extend service life. Upgraded clamping systems also reduce noise levels.

  • Steel clamping frame upgrades
  • Vibration dampening solutions
  • Noise reduction through structural improvements

Modern Materials

We use advanced magnetic core materials and modern insulation systems to improve transformer efficiency and reliability. Our upgraded materials meet current international standards.

  • High-permeability grain-oriented silicon steel
  • Advanced Nomex and pressboard insulation
  • Modern thermally upgraded materials

Why Regular Transformer Maintenance Matters

Neglecting transformer maintenance leads to costly failures, unplanned downtime, and safety hazards. A proactive maintenance program protects your investment and ensures continuous power supply.

01

Prevent Catastrophic Failures

Early detection of insulation degradation, hot spots, and core faults prevents expensive emergency repairs and transformer blowouts.

02

Extend Service Life

Regular maintenance can extend transformer life by 15 to 25 years, maximising your return on capital investment.

03

Reduce Energy Losses

Properly maintained transformers operate with lower no-load and load losses, reducing your electricity costs measurably.

04

Ensure Safety Compliance

Routine inspection and testing keeps your transformers compliant with SANS, NRS, and insurance requirements.

Our Repair Process

Every transformer project follows our structured five-stage process to ensure quality workmanship, transparency, and reliable results.

1

Initial Inspection

On-site or workshop inspection to assess transformer condition. We check oil quality, bushings, cooling systems, and perform visual examination of external components.

2

Detailed Diagnosis

Comprehensive electrical testing including insulation resistance, winding resistance, turns ratio, and dissolved gas analysis to identify all faults and degradation.

3

Repair & Refurbishment

Disassembly, core inspection and repair, winding replacement or refurbishment, insulation upgrades, and clamping structure improvements as required.

4

Testing & Commissioning

Post-repair electrical tests, vacuum oil processing, and oil filling. All tests performed to IEC 60076 and SANS standards to verify performance.

5

Quality Assurance

Final inspection, test report documentation, delivery to site, installation support, and commissioning assistance to get your transformer back into service.
