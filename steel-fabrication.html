What is Steel Fabrication?

Precision metalwork for gates, windows, doors, school furniture, and industrial components.

Steel fabrication is the process of cutting, bending, and assembling metal structures to create custom products and components. It involves transforming raw steel materials into finished products that meet precise specifications for commercial, industrial, and residential applications.

At Xpress Powder Coating, our experienced fabricators use advanced techniques and quality materials to deliver steel products that are built to last. From initial design through to final finishing, we manage every stage of the fabrication process to ensure precision and durability.

Our Fabrication Capabilities

  • Precision cutting using professional-grade equipment
  • Expert bending and forming of steel components
  • Professional welding including MIG, TIG, and arc welding
  • Assembly and finishing of complete steel structures
  • Design consultation and custom specifications

Materials We Work With

We use only quality steel and metal materials to ensure every fabrication project meets our high standards of durability and performance.

Mild Steel

Versatile and cost-effective, ideal for gates, structural frames, furniture, and general fabrication projects.

Stainless Steel

Corrosion-resistant and hygienic, perfect for food-grade applications, decorative features, and high-moisture environments.

Galvanised Steel

Zinc-coated steel with superior rust protection. Ideal for outdoor gates, fences, and structures exposed to the elements.

Aluminium

Lightweight yet strong, used for window frames, decorative elements, and applications where weight is a key factor.
